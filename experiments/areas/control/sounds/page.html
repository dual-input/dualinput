Stage 20b · Take-control's sounds · Round four

Pick a sound style

Dual Input plays a short sound when control passes from one pilot to the other, and another when a press to take control is refused. Pick the version you'd want in your headset, beside ATC and the engines.

Stage 20's stand-ins fit best, but each note is close to one pure tone. These keep their tune and shape, and fill out each note.

Under each style are sketches of the two sounds the voice handover adds after v1: the pilot flying says "You have control", and the offer times out if nobody answers within 5s.

Every sound is set to the stand-ins' loudness and encoded as Dual Input ships it, in Opus at 24 kbps. Start with your volume low. On an iPhone, turn off silent mode, or nothing plays.

Reply in chat with the name of the style you'd pick, or say none is good enough, and we look at a licensed pack or a sound designer instead.
