The camera as each core read it in the first flight with friends, 2026-11-11, from a replay of each pilot's recording on
2026-11-17, against the speed Follow drew the aircraft at and the frame rate of the sim's last Frame event.
The replay was a development build of the release 0.1.0's tree with two temporary log lines: avatars' camera_read logged
every read, with the frame rate the last Frame event carried, and flight-model's core logged every sample it fed Follow
and every report Lead gave. Both replays matched every checkpoint: pilot-b's 2,832 over 821,299 inputs, recorded on an
AMD Ryzen 9 9950X and replayed on an AMD Ryzen 7 7800X3D, and pilot-a's 2,662 over 446,841 inputs.

How it reads:
- Reads kept: the SIMOBJECT referential, within 6m of the reference point along z and 1.5m across, not on foot.
- The seat's eye is the median of this pilot's own reads while they flew, in the seat.
- dx, dy, dz are a read's offset from that eye, x left, y up, z forward, in meters.
- vx, vy, vz are the body velocity Follow drew at the read, from the samples' A:VELOCITY BODY X, Y, and Z 400ms before
  the read, in m/s, and fps is the Frame event's frame rate before the read.
- "-dz/vz" is how many ms of the aircraft's travel the shift is worth, against 1000/fps, a frame's time.
- "left along z" is dz after adding back vz/fps, what a correction from outside the sim would leave.
- pilot-b hosted, on the AMD Ryzen 9 9950X; pilot-a joined, on our development machine, whose sim held 30 fps.
