Take-control's budget: a single press to the cue, from the helper's stamp on the down, under
50ms when these ran, and under 100ms once the user had seen them.
Measured 2026-11-04 on our development machine, with no sim or game running.

The output stream's part, on the real helper's output thread: cargo test --release -p
dualinput-core-helper -- --ignored --nocapture a_sound_leaves, from handing the thread a
`Play` to a loopback of the same device hearing the sound start, of 40 plays of a quiet tone.

Device: Speakers (Focusrite USB Audio), mix format 48,000 Hz, 2 channels, 32 bits.
Its period is 10ms (default) and 3ms (minimum).
The stream's buffer is 1,920 frames, 40ms. At each event: 480 frames of space, 1,440 frames
queued, so a sound written at an event comes out behind 30ms of what's already queued.

  run 2    88,414us at p50, 96,484us at most

  run 3    82,436us at p50, 88,805us at most
