GetProcessTimes adds a whole 15.6ms clock tick to whichever thread runs at the tick, so the same audio run read the
helper capturing at 0.31%, 0.47%, and 1.25% of a core in three runs, each a multiple of 0.156%. The desk tool then
counted the helper's cycles with QueryProcessCycleTime against the time-stamp counter.
