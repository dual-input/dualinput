## Relay hosting for Crosscheck: options, costs, and limits (prices fetched 2026-10-09)

### Verdict

- **Small scale (the $10/month aim):** several single small servers fit, with 10 relayed sessions running all month inside their included transfer.
  - **Hetzner CPX02 in the EU:** about $8.09/month including IPv4, with 20 TB included. This is the best choice if most pilots are in Europe.
  - **OVH VPS-1:** $4.54/month with unlimited traffic at 500 Mbps, but that price needs 12 months paid upfront.
  - **DigitalOcean $4/$6, Vultr $5, or Linode $5:** these suit US or global pilots.
  - **Cloudflare Realtime TURN:** costs $0 below 1,000 GB/month, which is about 16,000–24,000 relayed session-hours. It also gives anycast latency. The catch is that the cores must speak TURN, and its per-GB price is the steepest once usage grows.
  - **Introduction server:** it can run on the same server at no extra cost [INFERENCE], or on the Workers Free plan, which allows 100,000 requests/day [DOC, https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing/].
- **100 relayed sessions around the clock:** that is 3.1–4.5 TB/month out of the relay and 6k–10k forwarded packets/s. Hetzner EU and OVH stay at their base price, Vultr costs about $6–19, Linode $16–22, DigitalOcean $25–38, and Cloudflare TURN $28–173. One vCPU is enough.
- **1,000 relayed sessions around the clock:** that is 31–45 TB/month, 94–136 Mbit/s each way, and 60k–100k forwarded packets/s.
  - Providers that charge per GB cost $155–422/month (Linode, DigitalOcean, Vultr), Fly $624–898, Cloudflare TURN $725–2,184, and n0's iroh $2.8k–4.0k.
  - Hosts with a flat price or a large allowance stay cheap: Hetzner EU about $16–38, and OVH $4.54 per server.
  - At this scale CPU becomes the limit, not transfer. The relay needs somewhere between 0.16 and 4 cores, depending on how much CPU each packet costs. So batch the system calls and measure on the server we'd actually rent.

### Traffic inputs [INFERENCE]

- **Payload:** 5,000 B/s per pilot.
- **Overhead:** 30–70 B per packet at 30–50 packets/s, which adds 900–3,500 B/s per pilot.
- **Egress per session:** 11,800–17,000 B/s, counting both pilots. That is 94–136 kbit/s, or **42.5–61.2 MB per session-hour**, against 36 MB for payload alone.
- **Ingress:** the same amount comes in, and most providers don't bill it.
- **Packet rate:** the relay forwards 60–100 packets/s per session.
- **Month length:** I used 730 hours. DigitalOcean and Vultr cap a month at 672 hours, which makes little difference here.

### When CPU or packet rate becomes the limit

Published measurements:

- **Naive Go UDP relay, bare metal** (one receive and one send call per packet, 128-byte frames, one core, Xeon w5-2455X): 335–380 kpps at about 2.6–3.0 µs of CPU per forwarded packet. [MEASURED-BY-OTHERS, 2026-09-23, https://github.com/romshark/toy-relay-afxdp]
- **Game UDP relay, one core** (unbatched, includes the game's own per-packet work, 88-byte payload): 35–40 µs per datagram, measured on 1 core under WSL.
  - 21,000 datagrams/s ran at 77% CPU with 0.43% loss.
  - 28,000 datagrams/s ran at 100% CPU with 8.9% loss.
  - On a production 1-vCPU, 512 MB droplet (provider not named), 5,400 datagrams/s used 23% CPU.
  - [MEASURED-BY-OTHERS, 2026-10-08, https://github.com/clintcan/3d-fighter-server/issues/17]
- **Cloudflare 2015, receive only:** a single thread on a 2 GHz Xeon received 197k–374k pps. [MEASURED-BY-OTHERS, https://blog.cloudflare.com/how-to-receive-a-million-packets/]
- **coturn:** I found no published pps figures. Its author claimed "thousands" of calls per CPU in an interview about a decade old ([webrtcHacks](https://webrtchacks.com/coturn/)). Its wiki performance page is outdated.

What that means for us [INFERENCE]:

- **Sessions per core at 70% load:** 2,333–4,403 at the fast rate, or 175–333 at the slow rate.
- **100 sessions:** needs 0.02–0.4 of a core.
- **1,000 sessions:** needs 0.16–4 cores.
- A virtualized shared vCPU is likely slower per packet than bare metal [UNVERIFIED].
- I found no per-instance packet-rate caps for DigitalOcean, Hetzner, or Vultr.

### Latency

- **Single-region relay:** a relayed round trip is roughly RTT(A to relay) + RTT(relay to B). A pair far from the relay pays both legs [INFERENCE].
- **Cloudflare TURN:** each client reaches its nearest location. Packets to a relay address enter Cloudflare near the sender, and misrouted ones travel over Cloudflare's backbone [DOC, [blog](https://blog.cloudflare.com/webrtc-turn-using-anycast), 2024-09-25].
- **Other anycast options:**
  - AWS Global Accelerator supports UDP and costs $0.025/hour (about $18.25/month), plus a per-GB premium from $0.015 and normal EC2 egress [DOC, [pricing](https://aws.amazon.com/global-accelerator/pricing/)].
  - Cloudflare Spectrum carries UDP only on the Enterprise plan [DOC, seen only on a preview mirror of protocols-per-plan].
- **Multiple regions:** with providers that pool transfer across regions (DigitalOcean, Vultr, Linode), three regions cost about 3 × $4–6 = $12–18/month base [INFERENCE]. Hetzner's cheap traffic exists only in the EU.
