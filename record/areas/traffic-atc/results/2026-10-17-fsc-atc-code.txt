FS Copilot's ATC audio, read in its code, 2026-10-17
====================================================

A research agent read the FS Copilot fork's ATC audio code in full for node
6.14 of the architecture plan, with the fork's record/traffic-atc/docs/ for the
reasons behind it. Paths are relative to the fork's src/. Coordinator.cs has no
ATC code; ATC is wired in Program.cs lines 122 to 133, and ShareSwitch registers
the packets.

1. Capture
----------

- WASAPI process loopback by PID, built by hand around ActivateAudioInterfaceAsync
  on "VAD\\Process_Loopback" with AUDIOCLIENT_ACTIVATION_TYPE_PROCESS_LOOPBACK = 1
  (Audio/ProcessLoopback.cs, ctor lines 93 to 139). includeTree = true is the
  default and AtcCapture never overrides it; ModeExcludeTargetProcessTree is
  defined but unused. The comment says "Windows 10 2004+ in practice, documented
  for build 20348+."
- IAudioClient.Initialize(Shared, LOOPBACK | EVENTCALLBACK | AUTOCONVERTPCM |
  SRC_DEFAULT_QUALITY, 2_000_000 (200 ms), 0, 48 kHz/16-bit/mono) (line 132). The
  capture thread waits on the event with WaitOne(100), keeps a 1 s scratch
  buffer, zero-fills packets flagged AUDCLNT_BUFFERFLAGS_SILENT, and raises Data
  (Loop, lines 147 to 171). GetBuffer's devicePosition and qpcPosition are
  thrown away (out _, out _, line 157).
- 48,000 Hz, 1 channel, 16-bit, framed at FrameSamples = 960 (20 ms)
  (AtcCapture.cs lines 15 and 16); OnPcm re-chunks WASAPI's packets into
  960-sample frames across callbacks.
- AtcApps.Known = ["BeyondATC", "SayIntentions", "Pilot2ATC", "PF3", "FSHud",
  "VoxATC"] (AtcApps.cs line 18). Names match the exact ProcessName, ignoring
  case, in one Process.GetProcesses() snapshot, keeping the oldest by StartTime
  (OldestOf, lines 53 to 74): "a launcher's child tree is covered by include-tree
  capture". Whether "SayIntentions" is SayIntentions' real program name is
  unverified; the fork's Q11 says SayIntentions was never tested.
- AtcHost.Target takes the preferred program if it's running, else the first
  known app detected, in Known order (AtcHost.cs lines 76 to 83): "A preference,
  not a lock". The dropdown lists the detected known apps plus "Other...", which
  calls AtcApps.AudioSessionProcesses() once: every process with an audio
  session on any active render endpoint, without PID 0 or FS Copilot itself,
  sorted by name (lines 81 to 113). The pick is kept by program name in
  settings.json as AtcApp. The dropdown shows only while the ATC toggle is on.
- AtcHost ticks every 2 s, refreshing the detected list and attaching or
  detaching only while hosting. Capture attaches as soon as the target exists,
  even while it's silent. The process is watched, not the audio: "loopback goes
  silent when its process exits". Tick detaches if the process exited or another
  app became the target (lines 96 to 108).
- No code handles the sim's built-in ATC. "Other..." would list
  FlightSimulator2024, and capturing it would take all of the sim's sound.

2. Encoding
-----------

- Concentus 2.2.2, CreateEncoder(48000, 1, OPUS_APPLICATION_VOIP), Bitrate =
  24_000, UseVBR = true (AtcCapture ctor, lines 47 to 49), 960-sample frames.
  Complexity, in-band FEC, DTX, packet-loss percentage, constrained VBR, and
  signal type are left at the library's defaults, which the agent didn't check.
  A new encoder is made for each attach and never reset between talkspurts.
- Mixer compensation: ReadMixer every 500 ms (MixerPoll, line 21) reads the
  lowest SimpleAudioVolume.Volume across the PID's sessions, and whether any is
  muted (lines 116 to 137). Gain is min(10, 1/volume), or 1 when volume is 0:
  MaxGain = 10f is "+20 dB; below a 10 % mixer level the audio is too quantised
  to rescue". Gain is applied in OnPcm, before framing. Mute is only reported:
  Status.MixerMuted shows "Capturing - muted in the volume mixer".
- The gate: per frame, after compensation, rmsDb = 20*log10(sqrt(sum s^2/960)/
  32768 + 1e-9); a frame is active above GateDbfs = -50; HangoverFrames = 15
  (300 ms); on an onset the previous frame is encoded and sent first ("the frame
  the onset started in", line 100). During silence nothing is sent: "~22 kbps
  while ATC talks and nothing while it does not."

3. Framing and stamps
---------------------

- AtcFrame(string Host, uint Seq, byte[] Opus), encoded as Host, Seq u32, Len
  u16, then the Opus bytes (AtcFrame.cs, Codec, lines 21 to 40). MaxOpusBytes =
  1023 - 16 = 1007; frames run "around 60 bytes" at 24 kbps.
- AtcHost._seq starts at 0 per app run and rises once per frame sent, pre-roll
  included (AtcHost.Send, lines 156 to 162). It's never reset, and silence
  consumes no numbers, so Seq is contiguous across talkspurts.
- No timestamp on the wire. The prototype carried one, Environment.TickCount64
  in ms at send time, "shared clock for the latency figure"
  (record/traffic-atc/tools/AtcAudio/Program.cs, Host.Send, lines 353 to 363);
  the product dropped it.
- 50 frames/s while the gate is open. The fork measured 16 to 27 kbps on the
  wire with UDP/IP headers, with 44 to 72% of frames sent through a ring tone
  with pauses (fork, build/log.md, "Stage 3").

4. Delivery
-----------

- Sent once, never retransmitted: _net.SendAll(frame, Delivery.Unreliable)
  (AtcHost.Send), LiteNetLib's Unreliable with no channel (Transport.cs lines 33
  to 39). The MTU floor is 1024; LiteNetLib throws an unreliable packet over the
  MTU rather than fragmenting it.
- HybridNetwork.SendAll sends on both the direct and the relay transport (lines
  112 to 118), so a duplicate frame is possible.
- The plan had audio on Sequenced; the fork moved to Unreliable after a shared
  Sequenced channel dropped a quarter of the traffic batches: "a late frame is
  still playable; the jitter buffer and PLC are better than any transport
  policy" (04-transport.md, "Delivery per stream"). Before relay protocol v2, ATC
  audio never reached a relay peer (build/log.md, 2026-09-08).
- No transport deadline; the receiver orders by Seq and drops a frame below
  _next while playing. LiteNetLib's DisconnectTimeout = 15000 ms, so a silent
  drop takes up to 15 s to show.

5. Playback
-----------

- A Windows device, never the sim: AtcPlayer opens
  MMDeviceEnumerator.GetDefaultAudioEndpoint(Render, Role.Multimedia) with
  WasapiOut(device, Shared, eventSync: true, OutputLatencyMs = 50) (AtcPlayer.Open,
  lines 87 to 104), through BufferedWaveProvider (48 kHz/16/mono, 2 s,
  DiscardOnBufferOverflow) -> VolumeWaveProvider16 -> WasapiOut. No device
  picker.
- The buffer rule (Tick, lines 160 to 189): frames go into a SortedDictionary
  keyed by Seq (Enqueue, lines 76 to 83), a duplicate overwriting the copy there;
  a talkspurt starts once count x 20 ms >= BufferMs = 60, 3 frames, with _next
  the lowest key; every 20 ms the player takes _next, concealing through
  Decode(empty), Opus PLC, when _next is missing and later frames wait; an empty
  buffer ends the talkspurt; a frame with seq < _next while playing counts as
  Late and is dropped. The depth is fixed.
- The play thread ticks on a Stopwatch every 20 ms with Sleep(2), and jumps ahead
  if it falls more than 500 ms behind. The device's clock drains the provider,
  and nothing corrects drift between the two clocks (the agent's reading).
- A gap the agent found, not in the code's comments: a frame that comes in late
  after a talkspurt ended is accepted, since _playing is false, counts toward the
  next talkspurt's 3 frames, and plays first.
- Every 2 s the player checks whether the default endpoint changed and reopens
  on it, because changing the default "does not [raise an error] - WASAPI keeps
  rendering happily into the old one". A playback exception closes the output,
  and Loop retries Open every 2 s.
- Volume linear 0 to 1, mute as volume 0, both persisted as AtcVolume and
  AtcMuted (AtcReceiver, lines 50 to 70; Settings.cs lines 23 and 24). Nothing
  ties volume to the sim's COM VOLUME.
- Measured: about 126 to 155 ms from capture stamp to leaving the speaker,
  against a budget of about 140 ms (capture 10 + frame 20 + buffer 60 + output
  50); at 5% loss with 80 +/- 30 ms of added delay, 52 frames dropped, 50
  concealed, none late (build/log.md, "Stage 3").
- Played, Concealed, Late, FramesSent, PeakDbfs, and SilentPackets are counted
  but never read, so the product logs no audio statistics.

6. The receiver's own ATC
-------------------------

- No code mutes, ducks, or detects the receiver's own sim ATC or ATC app. If
  both pilots run an ATC app, the non-host hears its own app and the host's
  stream.
- The host hears its own ATC normally, since loopback is a tap, and AtcReceiver
  creates a player only when the host isn't this peer (ctor, lines 25 to 39).
  Mixer compensation means "what the host sends must not depend on how loud
  they like ATC locally" (ReadMixer comment).
- No echo by construction: include-tree capture takes only the ATC app's tree,
  and the receiver plays from FS Copilot's own process, which
  AudioSessionProcesses leaves out.
- Frames from anyone but the current host are dropped (f.Host != _host).

7. Push-to-talk and the pilots' voices
--------------------------------------

- No microphone capture, push-to-talk, or speech-recognition code. The design:
  the host pilot's own transmissions aren't carried, the others hear the
  readback on their voice call, and "Capturing the mic on PTT is Discord's job"
  (01-design.md, "What it deliberately does not carry").

8. The switch
-------------

- Traffic and ATC are independent: ShareSwitch.Feature { Traffic = 0, Atc = 1 },
  each with its own claim set (ShareSwitch.cs line 21), reversing 01-design.md's
  one host for both (plan decision 1, 2026-09-06).
- Request(Atc, true) returns early if another peer hosts ("there is no
  take-over"), else adds itself and broadcasts ShareHost on Bulk. The host is
  the ordinal-smallest claimant; a losing peer stands down and the card shows
  "{name} is already sharing ATC audio." The toggle works only in a session,
  only while nobody else hosts, and doesn't need the sim; toggles aren't
  persisted.
- On the old host, AtcHost detaches and disposes the capture; receivers dispose
  the old player and build a new one, with a fresh decoder and buffer, for the
  new host; the new host attaches to its own target.
- OnPeers prunes claims of departed peers (the P2P peer list polls every 1 s),
  and a pruned host makes receivers dispose their player. When the last peer
  leaves, EndSession switches the toggles off and calls StopAll; hosting doesn't
  resume on reconnect.

9. Comments, dead ends, and measured numbers
--------------------------------------------

- ProcessLoopback.Dispose: if the loop is still inside WaitOne when the handle
  goes, "it throws on a thread with no handler, which ends the process; leaking
  one event is the better failure"; it releases the COM objects, because "a
  session that loses and regains its ATC app goes round this more than once".
- AtcHost.Attach: any exception shows "Audio capture needs Windows 10 2004 or
  later", even when Windows isn't the cause; there's no OS-build check.
- ILLink.Descriptors.xml: "Without this the published build crashes the moment
  ATC capture starts", since the trimmer removed the COM interop.
- In the fork's log: the mixer applies before the tap (25% mixer -> -12 dB, Q10);
  menu silence reads -90 dBFS, so -50 stays the gate; Concentus took 0.1 to 0.2%
  CPU (Q13); an elevated BeyondATC captures fine (Q18); Windows 10 (Q09) never
  tested; no device-capture fallback, ever.

Against Crosscheck's records (record/areas/core/04-core-helper.md)
-------------------------------------------------------------------

- Agrees: process loopback of the oldest process with its tree, a 2 s search,
  watching the process because exit is silent at -90 dBFS; dividing the mixer
  level back out, capped at +20 dB; 20 ms frames, 24 kbps VBR, the -50 dBFS
  gate, 300 ms hangover, and one frame of pre-roll; dropping a late frame and
  concealing gaps; a list of programs that own an audio session.
- Partly answers 04's question on format conversion: FS Copilot asked for 48 kHz
  16-bit mono with AUTOCONVERTPCM | SRC_DEFAULT_QUALITY and measured exact 20 ms
  framing, 250 frames per 5 s, on build 26200. Whether the wasapi crate's
  loopback client sets autoconvert is unverified.
- Differs: 04 stamps each frame with GetBuffer's QPC value for its first
  sample, where FS Copilot throws it away and sends Seq only; since it re-chunks
  packets into 960-sample frames, a frame's first sample usually falls
  mid-packet, so its stamp is the packet's plus the offset over 48,000
  (inference). 04's buffer adapts and counts samples against the device's clock,
  where FS Copilot's starts at a fixed 60 ms on a Stopwatch. FS Copilot plays on
  the default device with Role.Multimedia, where wasapi's "used by most
  applications" role is Role::Console (read in wasapi 0.25.0, src/api.rs, line
  137); the two are usually the same endpoint (unverified). FS Copilot polls the
  mixer every 500 ms and only reports a mute; 04 gives no poll rate and says
  nothing of a mute.
- Neither has anything for muting the receiver's own ATC, or for both pilots
  running an ATC app.
