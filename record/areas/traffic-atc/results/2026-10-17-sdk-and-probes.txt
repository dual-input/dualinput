AI objects in MSFS 2024, from the SDK and from our probes, 2026-10-17
=====================================================================

A research agent gathered what the MSFS 2024 SDK documents and what our probes
measured about AI objects, for node 6.14 of the architecture plan. SDK 1.7.3 at
C:\MSFS 2024 SDK\ ships no local SimConnect documentation, only
SimConnect SDK\include\SimConnect.h, so the pages below were read online on
2026-10-17 under https://docs.flightsimulator.com/msfs2024/html/6_Programming_APIs/.
Paths in our repository are relative to its root.

A. The SDK (documented)
-----------------------

SimConnect.h, SDK 1.7.3:
- SIMCONNECT_SIMOBJECT_TYPE: USER = 0, USER_AIRCRAFT = 0, ALL, AIRCRAFT,
  HELICOPTER, BOAT, GROUND, HOT_AIR_BALLOON, ANIMAL, USER_AVATAR, USER_CURRENT.
  The SDK doesn't say which are new in 2024.
- SIMCONNECT_OBJECT_ID_MAX = DWORD_MAX - 128; SIMCONNECT_OBJECT_ID_USER and
  _USER_AIRCRAFT are both 0, _USER commented "deprecated";
  SIMCONNECT_OBJECT_ID_USER_AVATAR = MAX+1 and SIMCONNECT_OBJECT_ID_USER_CURRENT
  = MAX+2, both "proxy value".
- The _EX1 creates add const char* szLivery after the title:
  AICreateParkedATCAircraft_EX1(title, livery, tail, airportID, req),
  AICreateEnrouteATCAircraft_EX1(title, livery, tail, flightNumber, planPath,
  planPosition, touchAndGo, req), AICreateNonATCAircraft_EX1(title, livery, tail,
  SIMCONNECT_DATA_INITPOSITION, req), AICreateSimulatedObject_EX1(title, livery,
  INITPOSITION, req).
- Also AIReleaseControl(obj, req), AIRemoveObject(obj, req),
  AISetAircraftFlightPlan(obj, path, req), and
  SimConnect_EnumerateSimObjectsAndLiveries(req, type).
- SIMCONNECT_DATA_INITPOSITION holds lat/lon in degrees, altitude in feet,
  pitch/bank/heading in degrees, DWORD OnGround, and DWORD Airspeed in knots;
  INITPOSITION_AIRSPEED_CRUISE = -1 and _KEEP = -2.
- SIMCONNECT_RECV_ASSIGNED_OBJECT_ID holds {dwRequestID, dwObjectID}.
  SIMCONNECT_RECV_EVENT_OBJECT_ADDREMOVE extends RECV_EVENT with eObjType; its
  comment wrongly names SIMCONNECT_RECV_ID_EVENT_FILENAME.
- Exceptions for AI objects: TOO_MANY_OBJECTS, CREATE_OBJECT_FAILED,
  LOAD_FLIGHTPLAN_FAILED, OPERATION_INVALID_FOR_OBJECT_TYPE, ILLEGAL_OPERATION,
  OUT_OF_BOUNDS, OBJECT_OUTSIDE_REALITY_BUBBLE, OBJECT_CONTAINER, OBJECT_AI,
  OBJECT_ATC, OBJECT_SCHEDULE, INTERNAL.

Pages under SimConnect/API_Reference/AI_Object/:
- SimConnect_AICreateNonATCAircraft.htm (legacy) "only works with non-modular
  aircraft"; the title "must be in an aircraft Preset", found with
  EnumerateSimObjectsAndLiveries; the tail "should have a maximum of 12
  characters"; "A non-ATC aircraft can be on the ground or airborne when it is
  created".
- SimConnect_AICreateNonATCAircraft_EX1.htm: szLivery "is only required when
  checking modular SimObjects"; the call "will check for the folder first, then
  the parameter name second", and when neither matches, "the default livery will
  be used." Exception: CREATE_OBJECT_FAILED.
- SimConnect_AICreateSimulatedObject_EX1.htm is "typically intended to create
  simulation objects other than aircraft": ground vehicles, boats, whales, or a
  stationary aircraft, with the same livery fallback.
- SimConnect_AIReleaseControl.htm: without it "the AI system and client may
  fight each other with unpredictable results"; to stop the sim updating
  lat/lon/alt/attitude, the page points to the FREEZE_* key events.
- SimConnect_AIRemoveObject.htm: "A client application can only remove AI
  controlled objects that it created, not objects created by other clients" or
  by the sim.
- SIMCONNECT_EXCEPTION.htm: TOO_MANY_OBJECTS: "The maximum is 1000."
  OUT_OF_BOUNDS is the radius error.

Data and events:
- SimConnect_RequestDataOnSimObjectType.htm: radius 0 returns only the user
  aircraft; above 200,000m gives OUT_OF_BOUNDS. The page doesn't say whether
  results come one message per object, or whether the user aircraft is included
  at a radius above 0.
- SIMCONNECT_SIMOBJECT_TYPE.htm: ALL is "all AI controlled objects"; the request
  returns objects whichever client or the sim created them, but not objects the
  AI doesn't control, "such as cars on freeways" (paraphrased by the fetch).
- SimConnect_RequestDataOnSimObject.htm, multiplayer: "it is not safe to use the
  ID number for the user aircraft", which "can change depending on several
  factors".
- SimConnect_SetDataOnSimObject.htm: the ObjectID can come from a _BYTYPE reply;
  "An exception will be returned if an attempt is made to write to a variable
  that cannot be set".
- SimConnect_EnumerateSimObjectsAndLiveries.htm retrieves "the list of spawnable
  SimObjects", "mandatory information" for the AICreate* calls, and sends the
  list "or a part of the list", or an INTERNAL exception.
- SimConnect_SubscribeToSystemEvent.htm: ObjectAdded "when an AI object is added
  to the simulation", ObjectRemoved likewise; no multiplayer event.

Simulation variables:
- TITLE "Title from aircraft.cfg."; LIVERY NAME "Name from livery.cfg."; LIVERY
  FOLDER; each a string of at most 128 characters. IS USER SIM "Is this the user
  loaded aircraft." No other model or livery variable for 2024.
- ATC ID 10 characters, ATC AIRLINE 50, ATC FLIGHT NUMBER 6, ATC MODEL 128, ATC
  TYPE 128.
- AI TRAFFIC STATE is a string, such as "push back 1/2", "taxi out", "enroute",
  "landing", or "" with no flight plan; CATEGORY is one of Airplane, Helicopter,
  Boat, GroundVehicle, ControlTower, SimpleObject, Viewer.
- FREEZE_LATITUDE_LONGITUDE_SET, FREEZE_ALTITUDE_SET, FREEZE_ATTITUDE_SET, and
  their toggles apply to "the aircraft (either user or AI controlled)"; the SDK
  documents no event that moves an AI object.

B. Our own records and probes
-----------------------------

p08 (record/areas/architecture/log.md, 2026-10-09; measured on MSFS 2024 SunRise
12.2.282174.999, the A2A PA-24 parked; experiments/areas/architecture/flight/client/src/main.rs):
- TITLE and LIVERY NAME read as STRING256, once; EnumerateSimObjectsAndLiveries
  for AIRCRAFT listed 6,297 entries, 16 of them PA-24;
  AICreateNonATCAircraft_EX1 40m ahead got object 56066048, TITLE "A2A Piper
  PA-24-250 Comanche", LIVERY NAME "C-FCIO"; AICreateSimulatedObject_EX1
  ("CROSSCHECK_Figure") 6m ahead got 56082432; all 66 calls S_OK, no exception
  (experiments/areas/architecture/results/p08-flight-2026-10-09-17-12-31.txt).
- From the .ndjson stamps (arithmetic): enumeration to its last message 5.8ms;
  aircraft create to ASSIGNED_OBJECT_ID 5.5ms; object create 4.8ms.

p28 (record/areas/avatars/log.md, 2026-10-05; experiments/areas/avatars/p28-many-figures.mjs):
- AICreateSimulatedObject_EX1("CROSSCHECK_FigureZ") on a grid, then
  AIReleaseControl and the three FREEZE_*_SET with 1 on each; up to 1,000 figures
  created, none failed; the module caps itself at 1,000, so the SDK's limit was
  never passed.
- 400 figures took 30.4s in p28's serial loop, about 76ms each, a loop of create,
  await the ID, release, three freezes, and one L: write.
- AIReleaseControl on every CROSSCHECK_ figure, of category StaticObject,
  answered ERROR (1) on parameter 1: 180, 800, or 1,000 exceptions per run, in
  p24 and p28, and on Marshaller_Stick in p01. Release gave no exception on GSX
  people of category GroundVehicle, nor on ProceduralCharacter, SimpleObjectSim,
  or aircraft. The freeze and the writes still held.

Other avatars entries (record/areas/avatars/log.md):
- 2026-10-02: GSX's people are listed among the ground vehicles as FSDT_ titles
  of category GroundVehicle, and show; 930 per-frame writes to a released,
  frozen GroundVehicle ran with no exception.
- p01's raw results (experiments/areas/avatars/results/p01-avatar-2026-10-02-21-05-51.txt):
  enumeration found 41 HELICOPTER titles, 3 HOT_AIR_BALLOON, and 934 ANIMAL;
  USER_AVATAR and USER_CURRENT each gave INTERNAL (45). ObjectAdded fired for
  our objects with eObjType ALL for simulated objects and AIRCRAFT for aircraft,
  before the probe had stored the assigned ID, and fired again and again for two
  objects the pilot didn't see. Created object IDs are large DWORDs that step by
  16,384.
- 2026-10-03, "Writes from outside the sim place an object in a moving cockpit
  but can't hold it still": SimConnect put seat and avatar objects about 2.3m
  behind a flying aircraft, and writes from a WASM module through fsVarsAVarSet
  on the object's ID held them.

p10 (record/areas/architecture/log.md, 2026-10-16;
experiments/areas/architecture/recording/capture/src/main.rs):
- RequestDataOnSimObjectType every 500ms for AIRCRAFT at 200,000m and GROUND at
  5,000m, with TITLE, ATC ID, and LIVERY NAME read once per new object; at EGLL,
  142 to 153 aircraft and 0 to 248 ground vehicles, 1,023 distinct objects in
  600s.

Design, not measurement: record/areas/core/03-sim-seam-and-wasm-module.md and
record/areas/architecture/02-code-map.md map create_ai and remove to the four
calls, give each feature a connection for each load, and leave unmeasured
whether closing a connection, rather than killing its client, removes its AI
objects. No run of ours has tested whether AI objects or their IDs survive a
flight load.

C. fsc-editor
-------------

- ../fsc-editor's docs/sim-vars/build/v1-log.md and src/main/sim/ hold nothing
  measured about AI objects, traffic, object types, or add and remove events.

Contradictions
--------------

1. AIReleaseControl: the SDK says it applies to "an aircraft, or other object";
   we measured ERROR (1) on every StaticObject, and the freeze worked without
   it. The measurement is the anchor.
2. The tail: the SDK allows up to 12 characters, ATC ID is documented at 10, and
   FS Copilot measured a cut to 9. The measurement is the anchor.
3. EnumerateSimObjectsAndLiveries for USER_AVATAR: documented, but p01 got
   INTERNAL for it.
4. "Must be in an aircraft Preset": AICreateSimulatedObject_EX1 created
   StaticObject and GroundVehicle titles.
5. The FREEZE_*_SET parameters are marked "N/A", while FS Copilot and our probes
   send 1, and FS Copilot read the variables back at 1/1/1.
6. CATEGORY: the SDK lists 7 values, and we read others: StaticObject,
   SimpleObjectSim, ProceduralCharacter, CharacterSim, VDGS.
7. SimConnect_Close is documented to remove a client's objects; only a killed
   client was measured.
